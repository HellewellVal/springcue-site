Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

SpringCue is run by Oliver Hellewell, trading as SpringCue. You can contact us at hello@springcue.com.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we collect your name, email address, studio name, town or city, whether your studio is open yet, its size, the booking software you use now, the plan you're interested in, and anything you choose to tell us about your studio.

If you fill in our studio survey, we collect your name, studio name, email address and your answers about how your studio runs bookings and cover.

Why we collect it

To contact you about early access to SpringCue, to give survey respondents their free month at launch, and to understand what independent studios need so we build the right thing. We rely on your consent, which you give when you submit the form or survey.

How long we keep it

Until SpringCue launches or for 12 months, whichever comes first. If you become a customer, we'll tell you how we handle your data then. You can ask us to delete it sooner at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your details or share them for marketing. Form and survey answers are stored by our form provider, Tally, which processes them on our behalf.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details, or withdraw your consent, by emailing hello@springcue.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

This site doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies. The waitlist form is provided by Tally, which may set cookies needed for the form to work.
